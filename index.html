A 3D spatial score of live sound

A song, built into a shape you can walk around.

Every point is a frequency your microphone hears, placed at a fixed spot in space — so similar pitches gather into the same cluster, and repeated notes build lumps. A thread traces the melody between them while the whole sculpture slowly turns. Whistle, sing, or play birdsong to grow it. Audio never leaves your device.
